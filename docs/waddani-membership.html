How do I renew my membership?
Go to My Card and tap Renew, or Payments to pay via Zaad. Your card updates automatically once payment is confirmed.
How does ZAAD payment work?
Select Zaad at checkout, confirm the prompt on your phone, and your payment is verified instantly — no need to submit a receipt manually.
Can I add family members to my account?
Yes. Open the Family section from Home and tap Add family member. Each dependent can get their own digital card.
My QR card isn't scanning, what do I do?
Make sure your screen brightness is up and the QR code isn't cropped. If it still fails, chat with us on WhatsApp for help.
How do I get help or report an issue?
Tap "Chat on WhatsApp" under the Contact tab to reach the Waddani office directly.
